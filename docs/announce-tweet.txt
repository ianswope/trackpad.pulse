Trackpad Pulse for Omarchy. A bar plugin that records what your trackpad does and tunes itself to it.

Optimize for my hand: libinput's custom acceleration curve has a physical x axis (1 unit = 25.4 mm/s on a touchpad). The plugin records your finger speed, draws the distribution under the curve, and proposes a curve fitted to it: Start where 45% of your movement is slower, End at the 90th percentile, gains adjusted at most 10% from overshoot corrections and re-strokes it detects in your touches. One change per pass. Every change shows its reason and the numbers. Nothing applies until you press Apply. Each applied change is logged with what the next pass watches; after 150 moves the next pass keeps it or proposes undoing it with the reason, and you can keep it anyway. It re-checks every 10 minutes and lights the button when the fit changes.

Bar icon: the pad itself, showing finger positions live. Off state dims it.

Counters: touches, taps, clicks, pointer moves, 2-finger scrolls, pinches, 3- and 4-finger swipes, rejected palms. Distance, touches, taps, clicks and active time for the last minute, hour, today, week, month, year, all time. Heatmap of touch positions. Touches per hour of the day. 7 days of history, recorded while the panel is closed.

Gestures: every 3- and 4-finger swipe and pinch Hyprland supports, each assigned from a searchable list of 137 actions: workspaces, windows, themes, backgrounds, Omarchy menus, screenshots, lock, audio, brightness, zoom, and any installed app opened fullscreen. Applies on selection via a generated hl.gesture Lua file and a Hyprland reload.

Report: this week vs last, per-day bars, which hand you use (from thumb and palm positions), mouse vs trackpad share by active time, opt-in auto-off (15 s of mouse use turns the pad off; a tap or 10 mm move turns it back on), touches per app, optimize history.

Access: no input group. Reads the pad if permitted, otherwise cursor-only, with an optional udev rule scoped to touchpads via one polkit prompt. Nothing leaves the machine. No page scrolls.

Built on Trackpad Plus by @davidfano, kept whole: per-device controls, libinput-validated curve editor, journalled state, his test suite still passing. Trackpad Plus was built on Andrew Kent's omarchy-touchpad-widget. MIT, both names retained.
